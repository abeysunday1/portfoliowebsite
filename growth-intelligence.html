The framework

From signals to decisions.

Analytics is useful when it changes a decision. This framework connects discovery, audience behaviour, lead quality and business outcomes—without treating vanity metrics as success.

01 / DISCOVER
⌕

Search visibility

Review search demand, technical accessibility, content relevance and how clearly a business is represented across discovery channels.

02 / UNDERSTAND
◷

Audience behaviour

Interpret engagement and journey signals in context. Separate meaningful intent from traffic that does not support the business goal.

03 / IMPROVE
↗

Lead generation

Connect landing pages, calls to action and follow-up systems so qualified enquiries can be measured and improved.

A transparent measurement standard

Every number needs context.

Public case studies will distinguish verified outcomes from estimates, observations and recommendations. No fabricated dashboards, unsupported claims or decorative performance numbers.

METHOD

Document the process

State the business challenge, baseline where available, work performed, measurement period and limitations that affect interpretation.

INTEGRITY

Protect the evidence

Use client-approved evidence and remove sensitive information. Add interactive dashboards only when they are connected to a real data source and clearly labelled.
