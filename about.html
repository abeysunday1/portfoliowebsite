Who I Am

Hi, I'm Adisa Abiodun Sunday.

I'm a versatile digital marketing professional based in Nigeria, working with businesses and organizations both locally and internationally. My work sits at the intersection of digital marketing, SEO, lead generation, content strategy, website management and AI-powered marketing — disciplines I approach as one connected system rather than a list of separate tasks.

I have experience across digital marketing, SEO, SEM, social media management, content marketing, lead generation, website management, email marketing, PPC, AI-powered marketing and marketing automation. Rather than focusing on isolated tactics, I look at how these pieces fit together to solve a business's actual growth challenges — understanding an audience, improving visibility, attracting the right prospects, and building systems that keep working after a campaign ends.

My approach combines creativity with strategy, and strategy with data and technology. I use AI tools and marketing automation to work faster and more effectively, without losing sight of the strategic thinking that makes marketing actually work for a business.
