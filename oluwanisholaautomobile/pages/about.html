About Oluwanisholaautomobile

Over 10 years keeping Lagos on the road — one honest repair at a time.

Our Story

Oluwanisholaautomobile started as a hands-on local workshop on Ojekunle Street in Papa Ajao, Mushin Way, Matori — the kind of place mechanics and car owners across Lagos already know well as a trade hub for getting cars fixed properly. Over more than 10 years, that reputation grew job by job: a diagnosis that turned out right, a repair that held up, a price that was fair. That's how a small workshop becomes the place people call first.

Today, that track record shows in a five-star reputation built over years of hands-on work and dozens of satisfied customers across Lagos, alongside "5+ years" of consistent, trusted service history. This website is the next step for us — a permanent home base where customers can see the full range of what we do, browse real photos of our work, and reach us directly, whether that history first found us through a referral, a workshop visit, or one of our listings elsewhere online.

What We Bring

Our team's expertise is experience-based, not just theoretical — built from thousands of hours under the bonnet, on the diagnostic scanner, and in the paint booth. We cover the full spectrum of car repair and maintenance: engine and transmission work, brake and suspension repair, auto electrical and A/C, panel beating, painting and body refinishing, upholstery and interior work, and vehicle inspections, on all makes and models including Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Kia, Hyundai, Ford, Chevrolet, and Range Rover/Land Rover. See our full Services and Brands We Service pages for details.

We also go beyond the workshop door. Our home and mobile mechanic service brings diagnostics, minor electrical repairs, battery and A/C top-ups, and emergency callouts to you, anywhere in Lagos — because sometimes the most useful mechanic is the one who can come to where your car actually is.

What We Value

  • Honesty on pricing. We don't publish fixed prices because every repair is different — instead, we talk through the actual problem with you and give a straight answer before any work starts.
  • Reliability. A repair isn't finished until it's done right. We'd rather take the time to fix the root cause than send you away with a temporary patch.
  • Willingness to come to you. Not every customer can get to Matori, so our mobile and home mechanic service meets you where you are, including for emergency callouts.
